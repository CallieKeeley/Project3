Project 3: Interactive strange places dataview

By Callie Keeley



The following is an interactive dataview I have created using the strange places dataset.

you may select from a variety of events and locations to view a heatmap of them. Hovering over points will give a description including latitude, longitude, and descriptions/dates where available. In the case of meteorites, they are hyperlinked so you can view additional information.

The goal of this site is to encourage you to find things near you, or not near you at all, that interest you and potentially spur future research.
